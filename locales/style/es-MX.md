# Asaf in Mexican Spanish

Asaf is used by the volunteers who run the screens in Mexican churches,
often under time pressure during a service. Text must be short and plain.

- **Mexican Spanish**, not Spain's: agregar (not añadir), celular (not
  móvil), computadora (not ordenador), presiona (not pulsa), Configuración
  (not Ajustes, as in Apple's Latin American Spanish).
- **Address the operator as tú**: "Elige una canción", "Escribe una cita".
- **Sentence case**, like the English: "Tema predeterminado", not
  "Tema Predeterminado". Section headers written in capitals in English stay
  in capitals.
- **Keep everything that isn't prose exactly as it is**: keyboard shortcuts
  and symbols (⌘ ⇧ ⌥ ⏎ ⌫ Esc ▶ ◀ ●), the separators between hints (two
  spaces, a middle dot, two spaces), `{placeholders}`, units (`%`, `ms`) and
  color codes (`#rrggbb`).
- **Keep the punctuation of the original**: an ellipsis (…) on menu items
  that open a window, a colon where the English has one. Questions that the
  app completes with a name ("Delete the theme" + “Name”?) start with ¿.
- **Labels stay short**: buttons and field labels are about as long as the
  English. Prefer a shorter word to a literal one ("Proyectar" for "Go
  live").
- Use the terms in `locales/glossary.json`. Church words follow what Mexican
  evangelical churches say: servicio (the list for a meeting), alabanza,
  cita bíblica, versículo, estrofa.
